# How to Use — YouTube to MP3

A short, friendly guide. No technical knowledge needed.

> ⚖️ Please only convert videos you own or have permission to download (your own uploads,
> Creative Commons, royalty-free music, etc.).

---

## Step 1 — Download the app

Go to the project's releases and download the single file:

**[⬇️ Download YouTubeToMp3.exe](https://github.com/ahm-raz/youtube-to-mp3/releases/latest/download/YouTubeToMp3.exe)**

It's about **100 MB** because the conversion tools are built right into it — so there's
nothing else to install.

Save it anywhere you like — your Desktop, Downloads, or a folder of your choice.

## Step 2 — Open it

Double-click **YouTubeToMp3.exe**.

- The **first time**, Windows may show a blue **"Windows protected your PC"** box (SmartScreen).
  This appears for any app without a paid publisher certificate. Click:
  **More info → Run anyway**.
- The first run also takes a few extra seconds while it unpacks its tools. That's normal and
  only happens once.

## Step 3 — Convert a video

1. **Copy the video link.** On the video page, copy the URL from your browser's address bar
   (or use the Share button → Copy link).
2. In the app, click **Paste** (or press Ctrl+V in the URL box).
3. **Choose a quality:**
   - *Best (VBR)* — highest quality, variable size
   - *320 kbps* — high quality (recommended)
   - *256 / 192 / 128 kbps* — smaller files
4. *(Optional)* Tick **Download whole playlist** if the link is a playlist and you want all of it.
5. Click the big red **Convert to MP3** button.

You'll see a progress bar and a log. When it says **Done**, your MP3 is ready.

## Step 4 — Find your MP3

By default files are saved to:

```
This PC → Music → YouTubeToMp3
```

Click **Open folder** in the app to jump straight there. To save somewhere else, click
**Browse…** next to "Save to folder" before converting.

## Tips & Troubleshooting

**Where did my file go?**
Click **Open folder** in the app — it opens the exact folder your MP3 was saved to.

**"yt-dlp.exe could not be prepared"**
The app couldn't unpack its tools. Make sure you have a little free disk space and that your
antivirus isn't blocking it, then try again.

**A conversion failed / red error in the log**
- Check the link is correct and the video is available.
- Some videos are age-restricted or private and can't be downloaded.
- Your internet may have dropped — just try again.

**It's slow**
Speed depends mostly on your internet connection and the video length. The app already
downloads in 8 parts at once to be as fast as possible.

**Can I use it on another computer?**
Yes — just copy the single `YouTubeToMp3.exe` to the other PC and run it. Nothing else needed.

**Is my computer going to get cluttered?**
No. The app keeps its tools in one folder (`%LOCALAPPDATA%\YouTubeToMp3`). To remove everything,
delete the exe and that folder.
