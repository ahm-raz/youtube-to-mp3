# How to Use — YouTube Downloader

A short, friendly guide. No technical knowledge needed.

> ⚖️ Please only download videos you own or have permission to download (your own uploads,
> Creative Commons, royalty-free music, etc.).

---

## Step 1 — Download the app

Get the single file:

**[⬇️ Download YouTubeToMp3.exe](https://github.com/ahm-raz/youtube-to-mp3/releases/latest/download/YouTubeToMp3.exe)**

It's about **88 MB** because the download tools are built right in — nothing else to install.
Save it anywhere (Desktop, Downloads, a folder of your choice).

## Step 2 — Open it

Double-click **YouTubeToMp3.exe**.

- The **first time**, Windows may show a blue **"Windows protected your PC"** box (SmartScreen).
  This appears for any app without a paid publisher certificate. Click **More info → Run anyway**.
- The first download also takes a few extra seconds while it unpacks its tools. That's normal and
  only happens once.

## Step 3 — Download a video

1. **Copy the video link** from your browser's address bar (or Share → Copy link).
2. In the app, click **Paste** (or press Ctrl+V in the URL box).
3. **Choose a format:**
   - **♪ MP3 · Audio** — just the sound (music, podcasts). Quality: Best / 320 / 256 / 192 / 128 kbps.
   - **▶ MP4 · Video** — the full video. Quality: Best / 1080p / 720p / 480p / 360p.
4. *(Optional)* Tick **Download the whole playlist** if the link is a playlist.
5. Click the big red **Download** button.

Each video appears in the list with its own status: **Queued → 42% → ✓ Done**. For playlists you
can watch exactly which items are finished and which are still going.

## Step 4 — Find your files

By default files are saved to:

```
This PC → Music → YouTubeToMp3
```

Click **Open folder** (or double-click a finished item) to jump there. To save somewhere else,
click **Browse…** before downloading.

## Settings (the ⚙ button, top-right)

Open the gear to turn things on for yourself — your choices are remembered:

- **Fast downloads** — uses many connections at once to beat throttling. *(Recommended, on by default.)*
- **Embed cover thumbnail** — adds album art to MP3s.
- **Embed metadata** — writes title/artist into the file.
- **Open the folder when downloads finish** — jumps to your files automatically.
- **Dark mode** — easier on the eyes.
- **Simultaneous downloads** — for playlists, download **one at a time** (default) or up to **5 at once**.

## Tips & Troubleshooting

**Where did my file go?** Click **Open folder** in the app.

**A video shows ✕ Failed** — it may be private, age-restricted, or region-locked. Open
**Show log** at the bottom for the exact reason. Other items in a playlist still continue.

**It's slow** — speed depends mostly on your internet and the video length. The app already uses
multi-connection downloads to be as fast as possible.

**MP4 won't play on an old device** — the app already prefers the most compatible format
(H.264 + AAC). Very old players may still struggle; try a modern player like VLC.

**Use it on another computer** — just copy the single `YouTubeToMp3.exe` over and run it.

**Remove everything** — delete the exe and the folder `%LOCALAPPDATA%\YouTubeToMp3`.
