# 🧩 Setup / Installation

Getting the app running takes about a minute. No technical steps, no sign-up.

---

## Step 1 — Download the app

Click this link and save the file:

**[⬇️ Download YouTubeToMp3.exe](https://github.com/ahm-raz/youtube-to-mp3/releases/latest/download/YouTubeToMp3.exe)**

- It's one file, about **88 MB**.
- Save it anywhere you like — your **Desktop** or **Downloads** folder is perfect.
- There's nothing else to install. Everything the app needs is already inside this one file.

## Step 2 — Open it

Double-click **YouTubeToMp3.exe**.

### If Windows shows a blue warning box
The first time, Windows may show **"Windows protected your PC"**. This is normal for any small
app that isn't from a big company — it doesn't mean anything is wrong.

1. Click **More info**
2. Click **Run anyway**

The app opens. You only have to do this once.

### First launch is a little slower
The very first time you download something, the app spends a few extra seconds getting ready.
After that it's fast. This only happens once.

## Step 3 — (Optional) Keep it handy

So you can find it easily later:

- **Pin to Start:** right-click the app → **Pin to Start**
- **Pin to Taskbar:** right-click the app → **Pin to taskbar**
- Or just keep the file on your Desktop.

---

## 📂 Where your downloads are saved

By default, finished files go to:

```
This PC → Music → YouTubeToMp3
```

Inside the app you can click **Open folder** to jump there, or click **Browse…** to choose a
different place before downloading.

## 💻 Using it on another computer

Just **copy the single `YouTubeToMp3.exe` file** to the other Windows PC (USB stick, email,
cloud drive — however you like) and open it there. Nothing else to install.

## 🗑️ Removing it

The app doesn't scatter files around. To remove it completely:

1. Delete **YouTubeToMp3.exe**.
2. Delete the folder `%LOCALAPPDATA%\YouTubeToMp3` (paste that into the File Explorer address bar
   and press Enter to find it). This holds the app's helper tools and your saved settings.

If you pinned it, also right-click the pinned icon → **Unpin**.
